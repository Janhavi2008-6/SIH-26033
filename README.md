## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution.

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmBridge – AI-Powered Direct Agricultural Marketplace

FarmBridge is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants and bulk buyers.

The platform aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries by creating a transparent buying and selling ecosystem while providing integrated logistics and AI-based decision support.

Key Features:

Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Marketplace: Farmers can list their agricultural produce with details such as quantity, quality, location and expected price. Buyers can directly browse, compare and purchase available produce.

AI-Based Smart Pricing: The system analyses current market prices, historical trends, demand and supply to recommend suitable selling prices, helping farmers avoid selling below reasonable market value.

Demand Forecasting: AI analyses historical purchasing patterns, seasonal demand and market trends to estimate future demand for different crops. This helps farmers and FPOs make informed decisions about production and sales.

Smart Logistics Management: The platform connects farmers with nearby transport providers. It groups orders from nearby farms and identifies suitable delivery routes to reduce transportation expenses.

Collective Selling Through FPOs: Small farmers can combine their produce through FPOs to fulfil larger orders, improve bargaining power and access bulk buyers.

Order and Payment Tracking: Buyers and farmers can monitor order status, delivery progress and payment details through a transparent dashboard.

Multilingual and Voice-Based Interface: The platform supports regional languages and voice-assisted navigation to make it accessible to farmers with limited digital literacy.

Innovation and Uniqueness:

Unlike a conventional agricultural shopping platform, FarmBridge combines direct trading, AI-powered price recommendations, demand forecasting and shared logistics in one ecosystem.

Its key innovation is the concept of collective order and transportation optimisation, where nearby farmers can combine their produce and deliveries to reduce costs while maintaining direct connections with buyers.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility of the Idea

FarmBridge can be developed using existing web technologies, open-source machine learning libraries and mapping services.

The project can initially be implemented as a web application and later expanded into a mobile application.

A pilot implementation can begin with a limited number of farmers, FPOs and buyers in a selected agricultural region before expanding to other locations.

Potential Challenges and Risks

Digital Literacy: Some farmers may find digital platforms difficult to use.

Data Availability: Accurate demand forecasting requires sufficient historical sales and market data.

Transportation Costs: Small orders may not be economical to transport individually.

Quality Verification: Buyers may have concerns regarding the quality of agricultural produce.

Trust and Adoption: Farmers and buyers may hesitate to adopt a new digital marketplace.

Strategies to Overcome Challenges

Provide regional language support, voice assistance and a simple user interface.

Use publicly available agricultural market data initially and improve predictions as transaction data grows.

Introduce collective transportation and order grouping for nearby farmers.

Include produce images, quality descriptions and optional verification through FPOs.

Build trust through transparent pricing, verified user profiles, order tracking and secure payments.

Viability

The platform can be financially sustainable through a small transaction fee, optional logistics service charges and subscription-based services for bulk buyers.

Basic farmer registration and produce listing can remain free to encourage participation.

## Impact and Benefits
Economic Benefits

Farmers can access more buyers and potentially receive better prices.

Consumers can purchase agricultural products at more competitive prices.

Reduced dependence on unnecessary intermediaries can improve price transparency.

Collective selling can improve the bargaining power of small farmers.

Optimised transportation can reduce logistics expenses.

Social Benefits

Provides farmers with better access to digital agricultural markets.

Encourages the participation of small and marginal farmers.

Supports FPOs and rural entrepreneurship.

Improves transparency and trust between farmers and buyers.

Promotes digital literacy through accessible technology.

Environmental Benefits

Optimised delivery routes can reduce unnecessary transportation.

Grouped deliveries can improve vehicle utilisation.

Better demand forecasting can help reduce overproduction and unsold agricultural produce.

Faster connections between farmers and buyers can help minimise avoidable post-harvest waste.

Overall Impact

FarmBridge aims to create a more transparent, efficient and accessible agricultural supply chain by empowering farmers, reducing unnecessary costs and improving access to fresh produce for consumers.
